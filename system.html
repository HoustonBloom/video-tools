Video tools

Five tools that read a recording so you do not have to watch it twice

Six tools that read and cut video. Each runs on its own, on any video, and none of them knows about a particular show or client.

01 · The problem

What it is

You have three hours of footage and forty seconds of it are worth posting. Finding which forty seconds means watching all of it, and watching it again when somebody asks for a different clip.

These tools do the parts a machine can measure. They measure, they narrow, and they never choose. Geometry comes off pixels, loudness off the file, cut points off the waveform. What is worth saying stays yours.

The thing that comes out

readthrough.html

One page saying who was up, when, what each stretch was about, and the lines worth cutting, with the frames embedded. Opens by double-clicking.

02 · The shape

What comes out

Every workflow ends in a file you can open. None of them needs a server and none of them uploads anything.

readthrough.html
What is in a recording: who was up, when, what each stretch was about, and the lines worth cutting. One file with the stills embedded. readthrough
stills, and a page to pick them on
Full-resolution frames pulled at the moments that scored, plus one crop per subject at a feed shape. A single HTML page comes with them showing every one, grouped and filterable, click through to full size, so you choose by looking instead of scrubbing. A manifest beside them records what was analysed and what was dropped. stills
moments.json
Every moment found, with the sentence it matched and the kinds that were looked for and not found. readthrough
a plan, then a render
The plan is committed as the record. The render is disposable because the plan is not. shorts
proof.png
One frame of a cut, before the whole thing renders. The check that catches an empty panel. shorts
03 · The jobs

The four workflows

A tool does one measurable thing. A workflow is the order to do them in, plus the judgement that is not in a JSON file. 2 of the four run end to end.

readthrough runs

Read a whole recording and say what is in it and what is worth cutting.

tighten runs

Remove dead air and verified filler from any talking-head video. Nothing is chosen, reordered or rewritten.

feature-cut not general

Cut one person's segment out of a recorded show and hand it over. Calls tighten.

composition blocked

Build a cut to a spine a person wrote.

Where a person sits

The full flow through the pipeline is twelve steps and seven of them are a person: saying what the source is and who is in it, looking at the proposed start, watching the feature, and sending it. Nothing here sends anything.

04 · The parts

The five tools

Each runs on its own, on any video, and none of them knows about a particular show or client. Each folder holds a README.md for a person and a SKILL.md for an agent.

transcribe
A caption file from a recording that arrived with none. a video file in, a .vtt out
readthrough
Read a whole recording and say what is in it: who was up, when, and where the demo was. captions, and the AI's read of the whole transcript in, an HTML page out
shorts
Talk footage in, a vertical Short out. a source video and a plan in, a rendered 1080x1920 clip out
tighten
Shorten a take without changing what it says. a clip in, the same clip, shorter out
stills
Photographs out of a recording. a video in, images, and a page to pick them on out
loudness
Get a render to the loudness it should be. any video or audio file in, the same file, on target out
05 · Getting going

How to use it

01
Install what they share ffmpeg and ffprobe on PATH, then pip install -r requirements.txt inside the tool you want. loudness needs node instead of Python. python dist/<tool>/check_install.py prints one line per requirement and exits 1 if anything is missing.
02
Get captions for your video Any transcriber that writes VTT. Normalise the audio to about -16 LUFS first. faster-whisper returns nothing at all on source-level audio around -34 dBFS. Silence in, silence out, no error.
03
Read the recording python readthrough/scripts/run.py --captions C.vtt --video IN.mp4 --style-from PARENT.html It ends with an HTML page or it exits non-zero. There is no third outcome.
04
Pick your cuts and build them Write a plan naming your source, segments and reasons. Then shorts/scripts/build_short.py. Proof frame before film: --proof 0 --at 2.0 --out proof.png. Look at it.

The shortest useful thing: point run.py at a caption file and a frame to borrow a stylesheet from, leave off --video, and you get a page in under a minute with no pictures on it.

06 · The map

What is in the repo

Two folders. video-tools holds the tools and is what becomes the repo. video-processing-pipeline is where they get run on real recordings.

inbox/
Video arrives here and nothing here has been touched.
runs/
One folder per run, holding what that run measured and produced.
outbox/
Assembled and ready to send. One folder per person.
experiments/
Tests of the pipeline. Nothing in here is sent to anyone.
workflows/
How the work is done. One folder per job, each with its own SKILL.md.
versions/
The method. No video in here.

A tool is built in the pipeline's _build/ while it is specific to one programme, and moves out to video-tools once nothing in it names a show. loudness moved 2026-09-05, stills 2026-09-06, the readthrough page builder 2026-09-07.

07 · Limits

What it will not do

It does not say who is speaking. A name is read out of the sentence beside it and travels with that sentence, because a transcript returns words and not speakers. a host's surname misheard, and a guest's name split into three. A name read by any tool here is a reading of a sentence, never a fact about who is speaking.

It cannot place a cut. Every time is a caption timing, wrong by tens of milliseconds. A cut point has to land inside measured silence.

And it publishes nothing

These tools write files. No upload, no post, no push. What leaves an account is your decision, per action.
